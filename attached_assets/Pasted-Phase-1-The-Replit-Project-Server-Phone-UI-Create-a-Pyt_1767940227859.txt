Why this specific prompting works:
The "Burst" Logic: By explicitly asking for "Rapidly press 'w' 50 times" or "Hold for 0.4 seconds," you replicate the video's behavior where one step equals a long stream of characters (distance traveled) rather than a single jerky step.

The Cooldown: Real walking has a rhythm. Without a cooldown (debounce), the sensitive accelerometer might fire 5 times for one step, causing the game to glitch. The prompt handles this.

Socket.IO: This provides the near-instant latency seen in the video (0:24), where the typing stops exactly when the phone stops.
